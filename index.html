About iSOFT

Excellence in Technology Education

Certified Training Academy & Software House

iSOFT IT Training & Software Solutions is a premier technology training institute located at Office 17, Jabir Flat, Abdra BRT Station, University Road, Peshawar. Under the dynamic leadership of CEO Engr. Shafiq Ur Rehman, our mission is to empower college students, university graduates, and IT job-seekers with hands-on, job-ready skills aligned with international standards.

Our academy specializes in Programming for Beginners (C++ & Python), Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA), Microsoft Certified Systems Engineer (MCSE 2025), Database Administration (Oracle & SQL Server), Cyber Security, and Data Science.

Every student enjoys access to physical computer and network hardware labs at our Peshawar campus, 1-on-1 trainer mentorship, and verified Google Drive 4K lecture archives for revision anytime, anywhere.
