    Access Protocol

    How Google Drive Lecture Access Works

    A comprehensive walkthrough for students on receiving, streaming, and accessing course materials.

    1

    Register With Your Active Gmail Account

    When applying for any course on iSOFT, always input your genuine Gmail address. Our backend permissions engine uses the official Google Drive API to link folder viewer rights to that specific account.

    2

    Payment Verification by Admin

    Once you submit your payment transaction ID or receipt screenshot, our admissions staff cross-verifies the bank deposit. This guarantees security and prevents unauthorized distribution.

    Access Notification & Drive Folder Delivery

    You receive an instant approval notification. Simply head over to "My Learning" in your Student Dashboard and click the button "Open Course Lectures on Google Drive" to start streaming immediately.

    Troubleshooting & "Access Denied" Errors

    If Google Drive shows an "Access Denied" or "Request Access" screen, it usually means your browser is signed into a different secondary Google account. Switch accounts in the top right corner of Google Drive to your registered email.
